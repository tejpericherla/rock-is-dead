Questions

FAQ

Is registering the same as a ticket?

Registering puts you on the list. We'll email you the details, and how to get in, before each event.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the night. Some are free with registration, others have a small door fee. The price is listed with each event above.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. Every event is 18+ or 21+ (shown on each event). Bring a valid photo ID, as no ID means no entry.

Where are the events?

Locations change. The area is listed with each event, and the exact address is sent to registered guests 48 hours before the night.

Can I bring a friend who hasn't registered?

Yes, just add them under "Guests" when you register so we know to expect them. Walk-ins without a name on the list may not get in on busy nights.

What should I bring?

Your ID, your confirmation email and good energy. Coat check is usually available, but travel light.

Do I have to dress up?

For themed nights, yes, the stranger the better. For everything else, wear whatever makes you feel loud.

Can I book Rock is Dead for my own party?

Maybe! Email us at hello@example.com or tell us about it in the "Anything else?" box when you register.
