FAQ

Is registering the same as a ticket?

Registering puts you on the list. We'll email you the details, and how to get in, before each event.

Where are the events?

Locations change and are sent to registered guests 48 hours before the night.

Do I have to dress up?

For themed nights, yes. For Halloween, absolutely. For everything else, wear whatever makes you feel loud.

Can I book Rock is Dead for my own party?

Maybe! Register and tell us about it in the "Anything else?" box, and we'll get back to you.
